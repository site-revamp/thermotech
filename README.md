# THERMOTECH | Thermotech Systems & Services LLC

## Website Overview

THERMOTECH is a UAE-based HVAC, refrigeration, cold storage and facilities management company established in 1985. The company provides technical solutions and maintenance services for commercial properties, industrial facilities, hotels, restaurants and other businesses.

The redesigned homepage positions Thermotech as an established technical maintenance partner, highlighting its HVAC expertise, specialist refrigeration capabilities and integrated building maintenance services.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Homepage Positioning

- Replaced the generic homepage messaging with a more specific presentation of Thermotech's HVAC, industrial refrigeration, cold storage and facilities management capabilities.
- Strengthened the introduction to communicate the company's core business offerings.
- Addressed the previous headline's failure to differentiate Thermotech from general maintenance providers.

### 2. Better Service Organisation

- Reorganised the service presentation into clearer categories covering HVAC, refrigeration, cold storage, MEP and building maintenance.
- Improved the visibility of specialist solutions, including industrial PCB repair.
- Reduced repetitive service descriptions and made the company's complete range of technical capabilities easier to understand.

### 3. More Reliable Trust and Contact Information

- Prioritised verified company information and genuine business details.
- Addressed the previous homepage's placeholder statistics, generic testimonials and incorrect international phone number.
- Improved the presentation of established business information and contact details to support customer confidence and enquiries.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Created a more professional appearance aligned with Thermotech's established technical and industrial positioning.
- **Layout Structure:** Improved section hierarchy and content organisation for easier navigation.
- **Service Presentation:** Made HVAC, refrigeration, cold storage and maintenance solutions easier to identify.
- **User Experience:** Improved information accessibility and the visibility of contact opportunities.
- **Brand Consistency:** Established a more unified presentation of company messaging and service information.
- **Trust and Credibility:** Focused on genuine company information rather than unsupported statistics or generic promotional content.

## Overall Direction

The redesigned homepage presents THERMOTECH as an established UAE technical services provider with integrated HVAC, refrigeration, cold storage and facilities management capabilities. The design focuses on clearer positioning, structured service discovery and more reliable business information to help commercial and industrial customers understand the company's expertise and initiate enquiries.
