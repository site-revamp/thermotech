About Thermotech

Engineering Support Built Around Your Business

Thermotech Systems & Services LLC is a UAE-based technical services company providing HVAC, refrigeration, cold storage and building maintenance solutions.

With a history dating back to 1985, the company supports commercial and industrial customers with services designed around the operation and maintenance of essential building systems.

From air-conditioning maintenance to specialist cold room installations, Thermotech brings multiple technical service requirements together under one provider.
